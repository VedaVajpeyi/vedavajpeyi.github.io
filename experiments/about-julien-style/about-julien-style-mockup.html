VEDA VAJPEYI

PRODUCT LEADER & SOCIOLOGIST

WORKS BETWEEN AMAZON ALEXA & BOOKS & BEDS

Veda Vajpeyi is a product leader and strategist who studied people long before she ever studied product. Her background is in sociology, not as a detour, but as the lens through which she reads every system she works in.

She didn't set out to study sociology. She arrived at UCLA as a biochemistry major, the safe and logical path, then wandered into an elective outside her major and something shifted: a course about how people, cultures, and societies shape each other invisibly, in ways that only become visible when you live between them.

VEDA VAJPEYI

She had been standing at that edge her whole life, moving between India and the US at six, watching the same human needs (belonging, safety, status) get expressed through completely different unspoken rules. Sociology gave language to something she had already been doing instinctively: noticing the gap between what people say and what actually governs their behavior.

She's spent the last decade in high-stakes rooms where the "right" path isn't obvious and getting it wrong has real cost, asking the questions users can't see. At Amazon, she works on Alexa, designing voice experiences for the unguarded moments of a person's day: the kitchen, the car, the early morning. Voice doesn't allow for performative product thinking; you can't hide friction behind a screen.

As the founder of No Desk Project, she built communities for the remote generation, believing location-independent work deserved more than a Wi-Fi signal. With an MBA from Chicago Booth and a heart for sociology from UCLA, she builds with rigor and empathy, for the whole, specific, complicated person on the other end.
